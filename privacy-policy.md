### Last Updated: [Insert Date]

### 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Apply mate** Chrome extension ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices.

**Our core commitment:**
- All your personal data is stored **locally on your device** 
- We **never** sell or transfer your data to third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes 
- Data is only sent to your chosen AI provider at your explicit request to generate your resume

---

### 2. Information We Collect

#### Data You Provide (Stored Locally)
The Extension collects and stores the following information that you voluntarily provide :

| **Data Type** | **Examples** |
|---------------|--------------|
| Personal Information | Name, email, phone number, address |
| Professional Information | Work history, education, certifications, skills |
| Project Details | Project descriptions, achievements, responsibilities |
| Resume Content | Professionally formatted resume data |
| Custom Fields | Any additional fields you choose to create |

#### Job Site Context (Temporary)
When you use the Extension on a job site, we may temporarily access:
- The job description or posting content on the active webpage 

#### Usage Data (Local Only)
- Extension usage patterns (stored locally for debugging and improvement)

---

### 3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored **exclusively on your device** using Chrome's built-in storage APIs :

- ✅ **Data never leaves your device** except when explicitly sent to the AI provider
- ✅ **No cloud storage** or external servers
- ✅ **No account creation** required
- ✅ **You have complete control** over your data

---

### 4. How Your Data Is Used

#### Primary Purpose: Resume Generation
The data you provide is used solely to:

1. **Generate your resume**: Your information is formatted into a professional resume document 
2. **Send to your selected AI provider**: When you click "Generate Resume," your data is sent to the AI service you selected (e.g., DeepSeek) to generate a tailored resume 
3. **Autofill forms**: Your saved information can be used to help fill job application forms automatically 

#### Data Transfer to AI Providers
The Extension transfers your resume data to the AI provider **only** when you:
- Select an AI provider from the settings
- Click the "Generate Resume" button

**This transfer is strictly necessary** for the Extension's core functionality .

---

### 5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Transfers

**We do not sell or rent your data** .

#### Limited Third-Party Transfers
Your data may be transferred to third parties **only** in the following approved cases :

| **Third Party** | **Purpose** | **Is This Required?** |

| AI Provider (e.g., DeepSeek) | Generate resume content based on your data | ✅ Yes, for core functionality |
| AI Service API | Process your request and return AI-generated resume drafts | ✅ Yes, for core functionality |

#### Prohibited Uses
We explicitly do NOT use your data for :
- ❌ Personalized advertisements

- ❌ Creditworthiness or lending determinations
- ❌ Any purpose unrelated to resume generation and form filling
